Recommended ACE Mode Trust Setups

Choose your active job and toggle how many Trust slots you have unlocked (3 to 5) as you progress through Rhapsodies of Vana'diel.

Core Trusts to Unlock Early

  • Valaineral (Tank): Unlocked via RoE tutorial quests. Uses Uriel Blade for massive low-to-mid level AoE damage and rock-solid hate.
  • Ayame (Skillchain Opener): Unlocked via Bastok quest (Lv. 30+). Automatically opens weapon skills that chain with your last used WS.
  • Apururu or Kupipi (Healer): Kupipi is free at start; Apururu (Unity) provides AoE cures, status removal, and self-refresh.
  • Ulmia & Joachim (Bards): Provide March (Haste) and Ballad (MP Refresh) songs to maximize attack speed and uptime.
  • Shantotto II (Magic DPS): Available via login points/special vendors; magic bursts relentlessly on every skillchain.

Dope Samurai (SAM) Blueprint

  • Unlock Quest: Speak to Jaucribaix in Norg at Lv. 30+ (Forge Your Destiny). Use Dancer's Spectral Jig (Lv. 25) to sneak safely through Sea Serpent Grotto.
  • Best Subjobs: /WAR for pure damage (Berserk, Warcry, Double Attack); /NIN for soloing NMs (Utsusemi shadows); /DNC for farming convenience.
  • Skillchain Loop: Pair with Trust Ayame to spam Tachi: Yukikaze (60), Tachi: Gekko (65), and Tachi: Kasha (70) for nonstop Light skillchains.
  • Level 75 Gear Goals: Hachiman set, Myochin Kabuto & Domaru (AF), Hagun or custom Sasanuki +1 (Smithing), Rajas Ring (CoP), Ochiudo's Kote, and Fuma Sune-Ate.

Epic Sword & Board Paladin (PLD) Blueprint

  • Unlock Quest: Speak to Balasiel in Southern San d'Oria at Lv. 30+ (A Knight's Test).
  • Why PLD Rules Sword & Board: A+ Shield skill, Shield Mastery (prevents spell interruption + gives TP on blocks), Flash, Sentinel, Rampart, and Cure IV.
  • Best Subjobs: /WAR for party tanking (Provoke, Defender); /NIN for soloing hard bosses (Utsusemi); /RDM for Phalanx, Stoneskin, and Fast Cast.
  • Level 75 Gear Goals: Koenig / Adaman Cuirass (Smithing), Hauteclaire or Excalibur (100 Smithing Relic Voucher), and Aegis or Ochain shield.

Inventory & Storage Expansion Guide

FFXI does not have wearable bags. Expand your carrying capacity using these quests and storage commands:

  • Gobbiebag Quests (30 → 80 Slots): Visit Bluffnix in Lower Jeuno (H-9). Trade 4 crafted materials per tier (or 1 Goblin Stew 880) with enough Jeuno fame.
  • Mog Sack (/sack): Buy from an Artisan Moogle for 9,980 gil. Talk to the Artisan Moogle again after each Gobbiebag upgrade to sync its size up to 80 slots.
  • Mog Satchel (/satchel) & Mog Case (/case): Accessible anywhere in the field. Satchel mirrors Gobbiebag size; Case starts at 80 slots.
  • Mog Wardrobes (/wardrobe): 80-slot gear-only bags. You can equip gear directly from Wardrobes without moving it to your main inventory.
  • Porter Moogles: Trade full Artifact/Relic armor sets + a Storage Slip to store 5 pieces inside 1 inventory slot.
  • CatsEyeXI Mog Vault (Lv. 75): Custom server system that swaps entire Wardrobes, Sacks, or Cases into extradimensional storage for a small gil fee.
